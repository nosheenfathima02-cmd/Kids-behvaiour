Super Mom

Good habits for Muslim kids, every school day

Children tick off their day on their own device: waking up early, the five prayers, homework, chores, healthy eating and screen time. Parents see every tick live, give stars and time-outs, and choose the rewards. A great week earns a trip the children choose.

Help

Linking a child's device: on the parent's phone open Settings → Children's devices → Link a device, choose the child, then on the child's device choose "This is my child's device" and scan the code.

A task is locked: children can tick a task only while its time window is open. Parents can mark any task done in the Today tab.

Google Classroom: some schools block outside apps on student accounts. Ask the school's Google admin to allow Super Mom, or add homework by hand in the School tab.

Prayer times look wrong: check the city and calculation method in Settings, and that the phone is set to your local time zone.

Delete your account

  1. Open Super Mom on the parent's phone and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings and scroll to the bottom.
  3. Tap Delete account and family data, then tap again to confirm.

This deletes your sign-in and all of your family's information right away. If you can't use the app, email [CONTACT EMAIL] from the address you signed in with, and we will delete everything within 30 days.
