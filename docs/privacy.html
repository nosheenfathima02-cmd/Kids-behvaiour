Super Mom Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

In short: Super Mom keeps only what a family needs to run its weekday routine: a parent's sign-in, children's first names and ages, and the week's ticks, stars and school tasks. There are no ads, no tracking, no analytics, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing. Children never create accounts. A parent can delete everything at any time from inside the app.

Who we are

Super Mom is a family app that helps children build good habits on school days: waking up early, the five daily prayers, homework, chores, healthy eating and screen time. This policy explains what the app stores, why, and the choices parents have. Questions: [CONTACT EMAIL].

What we do not collect

  • No location tracking, contacts, photos, videos, microphone recordings or browsing history.
  • No advertising, no advertising IDs, and no third-party analytics or tracking.
  • Children's last names, birthdays, school names or photos are never asked for.
  • The camera is used only to scan the QR code that links a child's device. The image is read on the device and is never saved or uploaded.

Children's privacy

Super Mom is designed to be used by children under a parent's supervision. Children do not create accounts, enter email addresses or passwords. A parent creates the family, adds each child, and links each child's device with a one-time code. By doing so the parent gives consent for the information above to be stored for their family.

Parents can review and change everything about their children in the app, and can delete it at any time (see "Deleting your data"). We follow the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU and UK GDPR, and the Google Play Families and Apple App Store rules for apps used by children.

Google Classroom

If a parent chooses to import homework from Google Classroom, they sign in with the child's school Google account on the parent's phone. Super Mom asks Google for read-only access to the list of classes and the child's own classwork (classroom.courses.readonly and classroom.coursework.me.readonly). It reads class names, classwork titles, due dates, links and whether work has been handed in, and stores only the title, class name, due date and link of work due in the next two weeks. The Google access token stays on the phone for that one import and is not stored by Super Mom. Super Mom never changes anything in Google Classroom.

Super Mom's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Classroom information is used only to show homework, tests and reminders in the family's app; it is not used for advertising, not sold, and not shared with anyone else.

Where the information is kept

Family information is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication), which encrypts data when it is sent and when it is stored. Push notifications are sent through Firebase Cloud Messaging. Google processes this data on our behalf under the Firebase data processing terms.

Security rules on the server make sure that a child's device can only see its own child and can only mark that child's tasks done while they are open, and that only a parent can change anything else.

How long we keep it

  • Ticks and time-outs are deleted automatically at the start of each new week; only a one-line weekly summary is kept, for 12 weeks.
  • Homework, tests and competitions are deleted a week after their date.
  • Everything else is kept until the parent changes it or deletes the account.

Deleting your data

In the parent app: Settings → Delete account and family data. This permanently deletes the parent's sign-in and all of the family's information: children, ticks, stars, homework and linked devices. Linked devices stop working straight away. If you no longer have the app, email [CONTACT EMAIL] from the address you signed in with and we will delete your account and family data within 30 days.

Removing a single child, or a single linked device, is also possible in the app at any time.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent information, and we do not share it with anyone except Google Firebase as described above, or when the law requires it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, show a notice in the app.
