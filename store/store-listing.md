**Full description** (max 4000)
<!-- play_full -->
Super Mom helps Muslim families build strong habits on every school day.

Children tick off their day on their own phone or tablet: waking up early, the five daily prayers, homework, chores, healthy eating and screen time. Parents see every tick live on their phone and stay in control.

★ STARS AND REWARDS
• A good day earns 2 stars, a full week earns 10.
• A time-out takes a star away, with a reason the child can see.
• Parents make the reward list: a picnic, the trampoline park, choosing Friday dinner. When the stars are there, the child picks.

🕌 ISLAMIC ROUTINE
• Prayer times for your city, with a reminder at each adhan time.
• Each prayer can be ticked only while its time is open.
• Qur'an memorisation goal for the week (surah and ayat).
• Optional Monday and Thursday sunnah fast for a bonus star.
• A short ayah or hadith reminder every day.

⏰ TASKS THAT CLOSE ON TIME
Every task has an opening and closing time. If homework is not done by its time, it locks and only a parent can change it, so children learn to use their time well.

📚 SCHOOL
• Add homework and tests; they become a task the day before they are due.
• Competitions get a countdown, a daily practice task and a progress bar.
• Exam week mode keeps the day to salah, Qur'an, study and sleep, with a 25-minute study timer.

👨‍👩‍👧 MADE FOR THE WHOLE FAMILY
• As many children as you like, each with their own colour theme.
• Link each child's phone or tablet with a QR code from your phone. Children never need an email or password.
• A shared tablet shows "Who are you?". Your own phone has a kids mode protected by your PIN.
• Everything syncs instantly between devices. A new week starts every Monday.

🔒 SAFE AND PRIVATE
• No ads, no tracking, no analytics.
• Only first names and ages of children are stored; no location tracking, photos or contacts.
• A child's device can only tick its own tasks while they are open; everything else is for parents only, enforced on the server.
• Delete your account and all family data at any time from Settings.

Super Mom is free.

### Target audience and content (Policy → App content)

- **Target age groups:** 6–8, 9–12, 18 and over. Because children are included, the Google Play Families Policy applies; the app meets it (no ads, no ad or analytics SDKs, privacy policy, no location, parental sign-in only).
- **Appeals to children:** Yes. **Ads:** No.
- **Content rating questionnaire (IARC):** Category "Reference, News, or Educational" or "Utility"; answer *No* to violence, sexuality, language, drugs, gambling; *No* to user-to-user communication and to sharing location; *No* to digital purchases. Expected rating: Everyone / PEGI 3.
- **News app:** No. **COVID app:** No. **Government app:** No. **Financial features:** None. **Health:** No.

### Data safety form

| Data type | Collected | Shared | Purpose | Required? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Personal info → Email address | Yes | No | Account management | Required (parents) |
| Personal info → Name (children's first names) | Yes | No | App functionality | Required |
| Personal info → Other info (children's age) | Yes | No | App functionality | Required |
| Location → Approximate location (city picked for prayer times) | Yes | No | App functionality | Required |
| App activity → App interactions (ticks, stars, time-outs) | Yes | No | App functionality | Required |
| App activity → Other user-generated content (homework titles, rewards, device names) | Yes | No | App functionality | Optional |

- Is all data encrypted in transit? **Yes**
- Can users request deletion? **Yes** (in the app, and the deletion page above)
- Data is processed by Google Firebase as a service provider; that is not "sharing".
- Not collected: precise location, contacts, photos, audio, files, calendar, health, financial info, web history, crash logs, diagnostics.
